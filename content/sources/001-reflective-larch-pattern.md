## About This Project

REFLECTIVE LARCH PATTERN

Here I present you a product inspired by my father who has been cycling all my life no mater the weather and no mater where he goes if it’s a photo shoot or a meeting.

This cyclist’s reflective jacket is to make the person visible in the streets at night.

The jacket is simple, a bit oversized to easily fit a sweater or down jacket underneath it and for unrestrained movements. It has big pockets to fit your gloves, a hat or it can be handy if you’d need to slip in some photo gear for a moment to free your hands.

It features my larch pattern from the „Color is life” graphic cycle.
